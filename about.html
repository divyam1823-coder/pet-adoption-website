Our Heart & Mission

Compassion in Action

Dedicated to giving abandoned, neglected, and homeless companion animals a second chance at happiness.

Founded On A Simple Belief

Started in 2018 as a small grassroots foster network, Paws & Hearts has grown into a community rescue and adoption center that has rehomed thousands of dogs, cats, and small animals.

We operate under a no-kill philosophy: every healthy or treatable animal remains under our loving care for as long as it takes to find the right forever match.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about adopting from Paws & Hearts.

Our adoption fees range from $75 (rabbits & small animals) to $150 (cats) and $250 (dogs/puppies). Every fee covers comprehensive veterinary checks, spay/neuter surgery, core vaccinations, microchip registration, deworming, and a starter bag of food.
Most adoptions are finalized within 2 to 4 business days. Once you submit an application, our counselors contact you for a phone interview, verify landlord pet guidelines (if renting), and schedule your meet-and-greet.
