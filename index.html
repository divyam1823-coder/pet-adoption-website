How Adoption Works

We make bringing your new companion home straightforward, caring, and supportive.

1

Browse & Connect

Explore our pet gallery and find a companion whose energy, personality, and needs match your lifestyle.

2

Submit Application

Fill out our quick online adoption application with details about your home, routine, and pet experience.

3

Meet & Greet

Visit our friendly shelter or meet with the foster family to interact and make sure it's a great match.

4

Welcome Home

Complete the adoption agreement, receive comprehensive medical records, and begin your lifelong journey together!

Why Adopt?

You Save Two Lives When You Adopt

When you adopt a shelter pet, you're not just giving a loving animal a second chance at happiness—you're also freeing up kennel space and resources for another animal in urgent need.

  • ✔ Full medical checkup, microchipping, and vaccines included
  • ✔ Honest temperament assessment and behavioral training support
  • ✔ Post-adoption guidance and lifetime community support
